# Changelog

Fidelity releases align with mathlib releases. Each entry summarizes the significant changes since
the preceding Fidelity release. Until the first Fidelity release, `Unreleased` records the current
mathlib baseline and major Fidelity changes.
When tagging a release, move its notes under the Fidelity tag and release date and start a new
`Unreleased` section.

### Fidelity changes

- **Inversion, exact division, and powers.** Partial scalar operations use `Checked.inv`,
  `Checked.div`, and `Checked.zpow`. Ordinary notation can infer unit evidence from the context,
  including nonzero evidence in a field. Nonnegative integer powers retain the monoid domain.
  `Rat.fraction` requires a nonzero denominator, and square matrix inversion requires a unit
  determinant. See the [checked operation examples](FidelityTest/Algebra/CheckedOperations.lean).
- **Signed extended-real division.** On `EReal`, `a / b` requires a nonzero denominator and at
  least one finite operand. This is the maximal domain of continuous extension. Finite numerators
  divided by either infinity give zero. Finite nonzero denominators give unit division.
  See [#84](https://github.com/KiringYJ/mathlib-fidelity/issues/84).
- **Extended-real differences.** Signed subtraction on `EReal` requires evidence that the operands
  are neither both `⊤` nor both `⊥`. On `ENNReal`, `ENNReal.monus a b` is the least `q` such that
  `a ≤ q + b`. See [#85](https://github.com/KiringYJ/mathlib-fidelity/issues/85).
- **Nonnegative extended-real division.** On `ENNReal`, slash requires a nonzero denominator and
  at least one finite operand. `ENNReal.extendedDiv` also admits nonzero numerators over zero.
  `ENNReal.divInf` is the infimum of the multipliers that make `b * q ≥ a`, while
  `Quantale.leftMulResiduation b a` is the greatest multiplier with `q * b ≤ a`.
  See [#108](https://github.com/KiringYJ/mathlib-fidelity/issues/108).
- **Euclidean quotients.** Use `EuclideanDomain.quotient`, `Nat.euclideanDiv`, and `Int.euclideanDiv`
  with a nonzero divisor. `Int.exactQuotient` also requires divisibility. On `Nat` and `Int`, use
  the named quotients in place of `/`. The remainder law `a % 0 = a` continues to apply on these
  carriers. See the [native quotient examples](FidelityTest/Data/NativeDivision.lean).
- **Polynomial and arithmetic APIs.** Monic division requires monicity. Finite root multisets and
  root multiplicities require a nonzero polynomial. The additive `p`-adic valuations require
  nonzero inputs, and `DirichletCharacter.rootNumber` requires primitivity. Use `RatFunc K` for
  the rational-function type and `F⟮x₁, ..., xₙ⟯` for the intermediate field generated by elements.
- **Number theory and arithmetic geometry.** Number-field Dirichlet density has a specifying
  relation and a certified value with equivalent zeta and logarithmic normalizations. Local
  elliptic Euler factors require ellipticity and finite residue fields and are invariant under
  changes of minimal model and passage to the completion. The split nodal criterion is formalized
  at a supplied singular point, including in characteristics two and three. The rational-node
  existence and torus identifications are documented from the source literature in the
  [reduction module](Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Reduction.lean). See the
  [density asymptotics](Mathlib/NumberTheory/NumberField/DirichletDensity/Asymptotics.lean) and
  [local Euler factors](Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/LFunction.lean).
- **Analysis and geometry.** Completion extensions require uniform continuity and a complete,
  separated target. Cauchy-continuity classes govern operations on completions. Added a category of
  formal multilinear series with composition at matching basepoints. Added analytic and meromorphic
  germs and meromorphic sections. Sections support restriction, gluing, and regular-locus evaluation,
  including for the Weierstrass function and its derivative. Ordinary Gauss hypergeometric evaluation
  requires nonzero denominators and summability. Bundle coordinate changes take overlap evidence, and
  unoriented angles require nonzero vectors.
- **Order and combinatorics.** Graph distance and girth have extended-valued forms with separate
  finite projections, and clique and independence numbers take values in `ℕ∞`. Fundamental
  matroid circuits and cocircuits require their existence hypotheses. Formalized countable
  cofinality and separation results for Boolean structures and stabilization of increasing
  sequences of set rings whose union is a σ-ring. See the [Boolean cofinality development](Mathlib/Order/BooleanAlgebra/Cofinality.lean)
  and [set-ring results](Mathlib/MeasureTheory/SetRing.lean).
- **Pushforward and probability laws.** `Measure.map` requires almost-everywhere measurability
  and `Kernel.map` requires measurability. Finite-measure normalization requires nonzero mass, and
  event conditioning requires a null-measurable event of positive finite mass. `ProbabilityTheory.cdf`
  gives unnormalized ray masses for measures finite on initial rays. Distribution constructors
  expose their parameter domains, including positive semidefinite covariance for
  `multivariateGaussian`.
- **Products of measures and kernels.** `Measure.prod` requires uniqueness for the rectangle law.
  The primitive and iterated product constructions have explicit names. Kernel products use
  `HasCompProd` and `HasParallelComp`. Measurable nonnegative Tonelli holds on the
  composition-product domain, including instances beyond s-finite inputs. See the
  [composition-product definitions](Mathlib/Probability/Kernel/Composition/MeasureCompProd/Defs.lean).
- **Conditional expectation and disintegration.** `condExp` and `condLExp` return almost-everywhere
  classes of versions, with `IsCondExp` and `IsCondLExp` available for version-level statements.
  Conditional independence and conditional sub-Gaussianity use these version relations.
  Conditional CDFs, kernels, distributions, and posteriors expose almost-everywhere classes on
  their uniqueness domains. A σ-finite first marginal supplies measure-level conditional CDFs and,
  for nonempty standard Borel targets, conditional kernels. See the
  [conditional expectation API](Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Function/ConditionalExpectation/Basic.lean)
  and [measure disintegration API](Mathlib/Probability/Kernel/Disintegration/StandardBorel.lean).
- **Validation.** Added declaration-level audits for retired operator dependencies, with separate
  reports on proof dependencies. Fidelity-specific regressions live in
  `FidelityTest`. The Lake test driver and fork-owned CI run it alongside `MathlibTest`.
  See the [CI documentation](.github/CI.md).
